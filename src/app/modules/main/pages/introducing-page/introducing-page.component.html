Introducing Stacksale Pay.

Stacksale Pay is an automated guest validation and payment system that will message your customers informing them a payment is required, provide a secure link to click on and direct them to a 3D-Secure payment gateway to make payment.

All aspects of Stacksale Pay are configurable and are customised for each customer — from the look and feel of each email to the workflow associated with each payment request. Stacksale Pay also supports on-demand payment requests for any other type of card-not-present payment requirement.

Stacksale Pay has customised solutions for Hotels and Tourism Operators but supports any business that needs to accept credit card payments securely online.
